# Phase 1 — Client Delivery

Version: `0.1`  
Date: 12 November 2026  
Source commit: `9da9bdc4540145aab8b4cefb2d65cffafcff8f94`

This release marks the final Phase 1 delivery of the Air Concepts Financial Admin Dashboard. It brings invoice visibility, organisation administration and cashflow forecasting into one workspace, with Xero remaining the accounting source of truth.

## Included in Phase 1

- **Staff access and administration:** email passcode sign-in, role-based access, and Director controls for staff accounts, roles and active status.
- **Organisation workspace:** switching between authorised organisations, with Director controls for organisation setup and Xero connection management. The workspace supports Services, Commercial and HotAS, with support for additional configured organisations.
- **Invoices and supplier bills:** read-only Xero lists and details, search, filters, sorting and pagination. Supplier bills are restricted by role. Sales invoices can be assigned to active staff within the dashboard.
- **Financial-year cashflow forecast:** a 12-month view with opening balances, money in, money out, net movement and closing balances, plus organisation and combined views for authorised finance users.
- **Monthly forecast generation:** explicitly generate and retain monthly forecast lines. Later Xero synchronisation preserves generated lines; eligible regeneration adds newly available items without placing the same document in multiple months.
- **Forecast planning:** manage manual cashflow items and staffing costs, review Xero items, and apply forecast timing adjustments, splits and exclusions.
- **Opening balances:** carry closing balances into the following month, enter an opening balance for an individual month, and show provisional carry-forward from the preview month.
- **Export and history:** export the forecast to the supported Excel template and review forecast activity and export history.

## Final delivery refinements

- Added editable internal notes to forecast transactions, including saving notes from the Money In/Out ledger.
- Compacted the forecast table and transaction ledger to make financial information easier to scan.
- Simplified the forecast header and financial-year navigation, with clearer section tabs.
- Added sign-based colours for opening, net and closing balances.
- Refined organisation headers and Xero connection-status indicators.

## Scope and handover notes

- Accounting records remain in Xero. Dashboard assignments, forecast assumptions and internal notes do not change Xero accounting records.
- The attachment panel and document viewer are deferred to a separate task and are not included in this delivery's interface.
- Chrome/Chromium is the agreed browser acceptance scope.
- This release identifies the delivered source version. Creating the release does not deploy the application or apply database migrations.
