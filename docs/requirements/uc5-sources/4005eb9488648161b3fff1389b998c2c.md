### Inputs and outputs

<b>Inputs:</b> Xero invoices, payments, credits, due dates; verified accounts contacts; calls, disputes and promises to pay.

<b>Outputs:</b> Current balance; follow-up history; customer response; recorded Director decision.

### Rules and exceptions

- Suggested schedule: −3 days, due date, +7/+14 emails; +30 call; +45 work-hold decision; +60 collection decision. Requires sign-off.
- Partial payments and credits change the remaining Xero amount due; a promise to pay does not settle an invoice.
- Disputes, key-account exceptions and payment plans require an agreed pause/review policy.

### Acceptance criteria

1. A partially paid invoice is chased only for the remaining Xero balance; a settled invoice is not chased.
2. A failed billing address creates an action for Financial Admin rather than silently continuing.
3. Each follow-up has an invoice reference, actor/sending evidence and date; a Director decision has a named owner.
4. Switching reminder responsibility results in one reminder stream.

## Rules and exceptions

### Work ownership, handovers and calendar

- vWork remains the system for scheduling field work; displaying its schedule does not grant edit ownership elsewhere.
- Google Calendar is the current site-visit tool; two-way sync is future scope.
- Suggested task escalation is day 1 owner, day 2 Service Manager, day 3 Director digest; conflicts with weekly digest timing need resolution.
- No confirmed SLA is invented; working-day rules and absences need agreement.

### Entity configuration, access, continuity and migration

- This page supports other use cases; it does not turn technology into a stakeholder.
- Xero is the ledger and numbering authority; synchronised read data is not a separate editable invoice ledger.
- Supabase stores forecasting data initially; SharePoint Excel is an output. Long-term store/region remain to confirm.
- Existing Services n8n run tracking uses Data Tables; do not replace it with Supabase locking/history merely because forecast data uses Supabase.
- Payroll and vWork stay in place; don't infer payroll automation scope from the Financial Admin's existing wages responsibility.
- Optional future entity expansion, dual approval and new integrations are configuration/design needs, not proof of implemented features.

## Acceptance criteria

### Work ownership, handovers and calendar

1. A handover identifies the outgoing/incoming people, next action and due date; role vacancy does not hide an item.
2. An owner sees their tasks across entities; a manager can reassign an overdue task with history.
3. A changed/cancelled source booking stays linked and cannot be mistaken for completed work.
4. Calendar filters and phone agenda show the correct events while preserving vWork scheduling ownership.

### Entity configuration, access, continuity and migration

1. A user with scoped permissions cannot view or change another role's restricted wage/payment information.
2. A record from HotAS cannot be written into the Services Xero organisation; linked IDs remain entity-specific.
3. A sample migrated live job reproduces its next claim and remaining retention from source history; unresolved balances are flagged.
4. Retrying a failed operation does not duplicate bills/invoices; invalid provider responses do not reach calculations or workbook writes.
5. Client reviewers can follow the agreed connection/recovery handover and identify the responsible support person.
